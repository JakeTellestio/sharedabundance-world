Prove one useful business

Start small: identify a useful digital product or service, find paying customers, and measure whether increasing automation can support sustainable operations.

What the first pilot should establish

  • A customer problem worth solving.
  • A practical product or service.
  • Real demand and a credible cost structure.
  • Tasks that AI can perform reliably.
  • Clear ownership, accounting and operating responsibilities.
  • A way to report results, including failures.

A sensible starting point

An online service or software business reduces the equipment required at launch. Exact product, budget, timeline and operating structure are being decided next.

Evidence before expansion

Publish what was tried, what it cost, what customers paid, what required human work and what the business learned. Expansion should follow operating evidence rather than a visual roadmap.
