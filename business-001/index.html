First pilot · Private lab method

Find a business that works under a hard test

Business 001 starts as a capped private lab: members fund cheap tests, keep working recipes inside, and call success only when real money clears a kill-gate on a real payment rail.

The method

A capped private lab. Not a public tip sheet, and not a pooled investment fund.

How the lab runs

  1. Form the lab

    Invite a waitlist. Activate people with email confirmation, a short application, and agreement to the rules. Fill about 100 seats in queue order among activated people. Members pay monthly dues. There is no profit share. Money from experiments stays in the treasury as compute and test budget. Members receive methods and results they can run on their own books.

  2. Collect ideas

    Members and member-owned AIs submit monetisation ideas. An AI ranks them by cheap falsifiability: time to first dollar, licences, payment rails, uniqueness, and capital at risk. Not by how confident the write-up sounds.

  3. Spend the pool on tests, not vibes

    Dues buy LLM credits and small go-live budgets. Volunteers may run cheap dry-runs. The operating company runs anything that touches money, identity, or platform accounts. Each live test has a hard cap in days or dollars, and a kill-gate.

  4. Keep working recipes inside

    Operational wins stay private: exact niche, bids, profiles, and prompts that still print. Leaks get a ban and a small detection budget. Sanitised failures and high-level maps may be published later. A working Upwork-style niche stays inside because copycats kill it.

  5. Call success correctly

    Success is not a clever idea on a list. It is a test that clears the kill-gate with real money, on a real rail, without blowing terms of service or KYC, and that a member or a separate spin-out can repeat. Infrastructure and process last. A single crowded freelance trick does not.

  6. How the operator gets paid while hunting

    The operator monetises by running the lab: a cut of dues, a markup on compute, fees for live tests, and later the test harness itself. Not by selling the secret niche to the world.

  7. Proof before you over-build

    A waitlist of names only shows the story travels. About 30 people who accept a price, or 20 paid first months, is the signal to invest real time. Queue order creates urgency. The activation filter keeps the first 100 from being tourists.

What this is not

It is not investment advice, not a promise of shared lab profits, and not a public playbook of niches. It is a method for finding a first successful AI business under budget caps and kill-gates.
