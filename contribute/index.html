Create a contribution draft

Choose an area and describe what you could offer. The draft tool lets you prepare and download your message.

Drafts stay in this browser and are not sent to the project. Download a copy to keep or share. A public submission service has not been configured yet.

Questions to work through

  • Which first business offers a useful, testable starting point?
  • How should reinvestment and distribution be balanced?
  • How should membership be verified while protecting personal information?
  • What legal structure can protect shared productive assets?
  • How should independent AI checks and human intervention operate?
  • What event should end temporary pioneer advantages?

Draft a proposal

Prepare a structured proposal locally. This draft is not a published community proposal.
