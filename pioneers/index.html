Early support. Defined terms.

The first business will require work, expertise and resources. Pioneers help with software, engineering, research, professional services, equipment, compute or funding.

Temporary rewards

Some early contributions may qualify for agreed compensation or temporary financial returns. Any arrangement needs published limits and a defined endpoint. It must not create a larger permanent entitlement to future member distributions.

The independence milestone

The aim is for productive businesses to cover their costs and finance continued expansion from their own results. The precise test for this milestone remains to be developed.

Recognition that lasts

Early contributors can be recognized in the project history. Recognition does not carry permanent control or superior member rights.

Current status

Funding arrangements and pioneer terms are not yet established. This page explains the pioneer path; it does not accept investments or offer financial returns.
