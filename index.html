Let AI do the work. Let everyone share the wealth.

Shared Abundance proposes a growing network of businesses that use AI and automation to earn real revenue. Available surplus would fund further growth and, over time, equal payments to eligible members.

The model

Build. Automate. Share.

Process overview
  1. 1. Build→
  2. 2. Automate→
  3. 3. Share available surplus
1

Build productive businesses

Start with products and services that customers choose to pay for. Hold the productive assets for the shared purpose.

2

Automate the work

Use AI and machines where they perform reliably. People handle the work, judgment and oversight that still require them.

3

Share the available surplus

Pay costs and obligations, maintain reserves, and reinvest for growth. The amount available for distribution would be shared equally among eligible members.

First step

Start with one working business

The first task is practical: develop one small business, earn revenue from customers, and establish whether it can operate sustainably with increasing automation. Further expansion should follow evidence.

Self-supporting growth

Growth that can support itself

A productive business can generate surplus. Some of that surplus can help build another business. As productive capacity grows, so may the amount available to share. Growth and payments depend on actual results.

Distribution rule

One eligible member. One equal share.

The proposed distribution rule is simple: distributable surplus divided by eligible verified members. Wealth, founding status or a larger early contribution would not purchase a larger permanent share.

Individual payment = distributable surplus ÷ eligible verified members

Oversight

Automation with oversight

AI would handle routine operations within published rules. Independent checks would examine important decisions, and people would retain authority over the rules and a defined way to intervene.

Proposed checks
  • AI operationsWithin published rules
  • Independent checksFinancial, risk and rule review
