Distribution rule

One eligible member. One equal share.

The distribution rule is simple: distributable surplus divided by eligible verified members. Wealth, founding status or a larger early contribution does not purchase a larger permanent share.

Individual payment = distributable surplus ÷ eligible verified members

Oversight

Automation with oversight

AI handles routine operations within published rules. Independent checks examine important decisions, and people retain authority over the rules and a defined way to intervene.
