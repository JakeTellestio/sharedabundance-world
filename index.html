UHI - Universal High Income

Let AI do the work. Let everyone share the wealth.

Shared Abundance is a worldwide, people-controlled system designed to gradually build an automated economy whose wealth belongs equally to everyone.

See how it works Help build it

It isn’t a government, political party, charity, cryptocurrency, or traditional company. It is an attempt to build a new economic layer alongside the existing economy and allow that new economy to grow until it can provide every person with a Universal High Income (UHI).

The important difference from UBI is the ambition. UBI normally asks, “How do we guarantee everyone enough money to live?” Shared Abundance asks:

The model

Build. Automate. Share.

Process overview
  1. 1. Build→
  2. 2. Automate→
  3. 3. Share available surplus
1

Build productive businesses

Start with products and services that customers choose to pay for.

2

Automate the work

Use AI and machines where they work well. People still do the jobs and judgment the machines can’t.

3

Share the available surplus

Pay costs and obligations. The amount available for distribution is shared equally among members.

First step

Start with one working business

The first task is practical: develop one small business, earn revenue from customers, and establish whether it can operate sustainably.

Explore Business 001

Distribution rule

One eligible member. One equal share.

The sharing rule is simple: leftover money, split equally among members. Being a founder, or putting in more early money does not buy a permanently bigger share.

Individual payment = leftover money ÷ members

Oversight

Automation with oversight

AI does the everyday work under clear rules. Other systems check the big decisions. People set the rules and can step in when needed.
